## SDK research is not a production fork

The first-notification loss43.5 was causally observed in the sample-server SDK:
a closed old Publish request consumed the notification. An isolated server-only
patch passed43.5→44.5; a baseline pass also occurred, so the manual diagnostic is
timing-sensitive and is not a required CI counterfactual gate.

A distinct real wire-loss probe proved that the pinned client did not
Republish automatically; public Republish returned retained52.5 without its
normal callback, while53.5 arrived normally. Library-only candidate patches and
before/after stands are stored separately. The candidate remains research:
ordering windows, failure delivery, cancellation, Transfer/NewSession, secure
endpoints and the App identity-ready boundary are not complete-history proofs.
Production Cargo pins and the App callback gate are unchanged by these patches.
